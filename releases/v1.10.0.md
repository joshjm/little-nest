# Little Nest 1.10.0

## At a glance

- The top of **Today** now shows the next thing coming up in large text, like
  the Pixel "At a glance" view: next milk feed, nap, wet nappy, dirty nappy or
  reminder. The next few follow underneath. Tap any of them for details.

## Next wet and dirty nappy predictions

- A small random forest (30 decision trees) learns from your own logs, on the
  phone. It looks at feeds, bottle amounts, solids, sleep and time of day.
  There is no online or paid service.
- Babies change as they grow, so only the last 30 days are used and newer days
  count more (a day 3 days older counts half).
- Before showing a time, it checks itself against your newest days. If a simple
  average would have done better, it uses that. If it would still be more than
  4 hours off, it shows how often nappies usually come instead of a clock time.
- Needs at least 8 wet or 5 dirty nappies across 3 days.

These are guesses from your logs, not health advice. Follow baby's cues.

No data, storage, import or sync formats changed. Install this same-key APK
over the existing app without uninstalling; Obtainium users can update as usual.
