# Little Nest 1.10.0

- New **Plan** tab brings sleep and feeding planning into one place.
- Set optional daily goals for total sleep hours, daytime naps and milk-feed
  sessions. See logged totals since local midnight, including running sleep
  and excluding pauses. Top-ups and split naps count together.
- See interval estimates, daily clock schedules and history-adjusted timings,
  with explanations when there is not enough usable history.
- Learn an evening bedtime window from at least four previous days explicitly
  logged as **Night sleep**. This does not generate bedtime alerts or predict
  sleeping through the night.
- Read age-specific sleep, nap and feeding context, with NHS, CDC and HSE
  source links. Missing birth dates are never guessed.

Goals are personal preferences, not medical targets. They are per baby, per
phone, and are not synced or included in backups. They do not change predictions
or existing advance alerts. Today's totals are a partial calendar day, not a
complete 24-hour comparison. Nap counting includes explicitly labelled naps
and otherwise unlabelled daytime sleep; Night sleep is excluded.

Patterns can be unreliable when logs are missing or routines change. Follow
baby's cues and your clinician's care plan; never delay feeding, restrict intake
or add care to meet a number. Guidance uses age from birth, not adjusted age.
No intake or sleep-debt calculation is made.

Same application ID and original signing certificate; install over the existing
app to retain data. Back up before updating. Obtainium can use `LittleNest.apk`
and the portable `SHA256SUMS`.
