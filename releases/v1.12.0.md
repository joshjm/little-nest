# Little Nest 1.12.0

A little more life in your nest, with the same calm, private care tracking.

- Original bird-and-nest illustrations on Today, Plan, Family and first use,
  with a moonlit version for night feeds.
- More recognizable activity artwork: feeding, food, bath, tummy time,
  milestones, mood, self-care and individual health activities.
- Larger illustrated activity icons, plus matching symbols in timers and
  upcoming-care rows.
- A brief wing flutter and heart after logging care on Today, and a gentle
  selected-tab response. Both respect Android's Remove animations setting.
- Fresh screenshots use only synthetic demo records.

No looping GIFs, downloads, sounds, additional permissions or animation
dependencies. Tracking, predictions, imports, backups and sync formats are
unchanged. Planning estimates remain estimates, not deadlines.

Install over your existing app to retain records. Android 9 or newer.
