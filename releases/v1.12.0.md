# Little Nest 1.12.0

## A simpler Today screen

- **At a glance** is now the one place on Today for what's next: milk feed,
  nap, wet nappy and dirty nappy. Before, feed and nap times also appeared in
  a second **Coming up** card with three different estimates each.
- The detailed **Coming up** comparison is now only on the **Plan** tab. Tap
  any At a glance row to see the same comparison and evidence.
- **Care plan & advance alerts** is on **Plan**, and in the details of each
  At a glance row.
- Reminders and appointments now show only in **Upcoming reminders & plans**,
  instead of in both places.

No data, storage, import, sync or alert behaviour changed. Install this
same-key APK over the existing app without uninstalling; Obtainium users can
update as usual.
